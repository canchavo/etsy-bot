Privacy Policy

Nuvira Studio (NuviraStudioDigital) · Last updated: 14 October 2026

This policy explains how the Nuvira Studio publishing tool ("the app") handles data. The app is a private, single-user tool operated by the owner of the Etsy shop NuviraStudioDigital and the Pinterest account @nuvirastudiodigital. It is not offered to other users.

What the app does

The app creates Pins on the owner's own Pinterest account to promote the owner's own digital products sold on Etsy, and lists the owner's own boards so it can choose where to publish a Pin.

Data we access

  • Pinterest: the owner's account ID, boards and Pins, accessed only through the official Pinterest API with the owner's authorization.
  • Etsy: the owner's own shop listings (titles, images, links).

The app does not access, collect or store data about any other Pinterest user.

How data is stored

Access tokens and product records are stored encrypted and are used only to publish the owner's content. No data is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising profiles.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept only as long as needed to run the tool. Access can be revoked at any time from Pinterest settings (Security → Apps), after which the stored token stops working and is deleted.

Contact

Questions about this policy: send a message to the shop NuviraStudioDigital on Etsy.
